## Before You Start — Download & Setup

1. Download **CapCut** on your phone (free on iOS & Android)
2. Open CapCut → tap **"New Project"**
3. For each video, you'll select your clips or photos from your gallery
4. **Tip:** You can use the Canva designs we created as still image clips in CapCut

## Video 1 — Brand Introduction Reel (15s)

**Goal:** Introduce Lion Jobs Agency to new audiences

### Script (read aloud to camera or use text overlays only)
```
[0–3s]  "Looking for your dream job in Myanmar?"
[3–8s]  "Lion Jobs Agency connects ambitious professionals
         with Myanmar's top companies."
[8–12s] "AI-matched. Human-verified. 100% Free."
[12–15s] "Visit lion-jobs-platform.vercel.app — Link in bio!"
```

### Step-by-Step in CapCut

**Step 1 — Create project**
- Open CapCut → New Project
- Select 3–4 photos (use the Canva Instagram post design, office photos, or any professional photos)
- Tap "Add"

**Step 2 — Set canvas size**
- Tap the "16:9" button in the top bar → select **9:16** (for Reels/TikTok)

**Step 3 — Add text overlays**
- Tap each clip → tap **"Text"** → **"Add Text"**
- Line 1 (0–3s): Type **"🦁 Lion Jobs Agency"**
  - Font: choose **"Montserrat"** or **"Poppins"**
  - Color: **White** with **Navy blue** shadow
- Line 2 (3–8s): Type **"Myanmar's #1 AI Recruitment Platform"**
  - Make text large, center it
- Line 3 (8–12s): Type **"AI-Matched · Free for Candidates"**
- Line 4 (12–15s): Type **"Link in Bio → Apply Now!"**

**Step 4 — Add transitions**
- Tap between clips → tap **"Transition"**
- Choose **"Fade"** or **"Slide"** — keep it clean, not flashy

**Step 5 — Add music**
- Tap **"Audio"** → **"Music"** → search **"corporate inspiring"**
- Volume: set to **15%** (background only, don't overpower)

**Step 6 — Add brand colors with filters**
- Tap a clip → **"Adjust"** → increase **Contrast +15**, **Saturation +10**, **Brightness +5**
- This gives a premium, cinematic feel

**Step 7 — Export**
- Tap **"Export"** (top right) → **1080p** → **30fps**
- Save to gallery → upload to Instagram/TikTok

## Video 2 — "We're Hiring" Job Announcement (12s)

**Goal:** Announce open positions, drive applications

### Script
```
[0–2s]  Big text: "🔥 WE'RE HIRING"
[2–6s]  "Top roles open NOW in Yangon:"
[6–10s] List: "• Marketing Manager
               • Software Engineer
               • Sales Executive"
[10–12s] "Apply FREE → lion-jobs-platform.vercel.app"
```

### CapCut Steps

**Step 1** — New Project → add a dark blue/navy background image (use the Canva Facebook post design)

**Step 2 — Animated text (this is the key!)**
- Tap **"Text"** → add **"WE'RE HIRING"**
- Make it VERY LARGE, bold, white
- Tap the text → **"Animation"** → choose **"Bounce In"** or **"Scale In"**
- Duration: 0.5s animation

**Step 3 — Job list text**
- Add 3 separate text boxes (one per job title)
- Set each to appear 0.5s after the previous one (staggered entry)
- Use **"Slide from Left"** animation for each

**Step 4 — Gold accent**
- Tap **"Stickers"** → search **"gold line"** or **"gold divider"**
- Place a horizontal gold line between the headline and job list

**Step 5 — CTA text**
- Last text: **"Apply FREE → See Link in Bio"**
- Color: **Gold (#D4AF37)**
- Animation: **"Fade In"**

**Step 6 — Music**
- Search **"upbeat corporate"** → pick something energetic
- Volume: **20%**

**Goal:** Build trust with a real placement story

```
         "I sent my CV to Lion Jobs on Monday.
          They called me by Wednesday.
          I got the offer on Friday."
[18–23s] Text: "Lion Jobs — Myanmar's Most Trusted
                Recruitment Agency"
[23–25s] CTA: "Your story could be next → Link in bio"
```

### CapCut Steps

**Step 1** — Use a real photo of your placed candidate (or use a stock photo of a professional)

**Step 2 — Ken Burns zoom effect**
- Select the photo → **"Basic"** → **"Scale"** → set start scale 100%, end scale 110%
- This gives a cinematic slow-zoom feeling to still photos

**Step 3 — Quote text**
- Use **"Typewriter"** animation for the quote text
- Font: elegant serif like **"Playfair Display"**
- Color: White with subtle dark overlay behind text for readability

**Step 4 — Emotional music**
- Search **"emotional piano"** or **"inspiring orchestral"**
- Volume: **25%** for this one (emotional impact)

## Video 5 — Behind-the-Scenes Culture Reel (15s)

**Goal:** Humanize the brand, show the team

### Script
```
[0–4s]  "Behind the scenes at Lion Jobs Agency 🦁"
[4–10s] Short clips of: your office, team members,
         phones ringing, reviewing CVs
         (even just selfie-style phone clips work!)
[10–13s] Text: "Every day we help Myanmar's
                best find their next chapter."
[13–15s] "Follow us for new jobs every week!"
```

### CapCut Steps

**Step 1** — Record 4–5 quick 2–3 second clips on your phone:
- Your desk/workspace
- You reviewing a CV or making a call
- Outside your office building
- (Even casual clips work — authenticity beats production value for this type)

**Step 2 — Speed up clips**
- Select each clip → **"Speed"** → set to **1.5x** (keeps energy high)

**Step 3 — Add beat sync**
- Add an upbeat track → tap **"Auto Beat Sync"** → CapCut will auto-cut clips to the music beat

**Step 4 — Branded end card**
- Last 3s: use the Canva Instagram Post design as a static end card
- Add text: **"New Jobs Every Week · Follow for Updates"**

## Universal Tips for All Videos

### Text Rules
- **Never use more than 2 font styles** in one video
- **Recommended fonts in CapCut:** Montserrat Bold (headlines), Poppins Regular (body)
- **Colors:** White text on dark background, Gold (#D4AF37) for CTAs only
- **Text size:** Make it BIGGER than you think — people watch on phones

### Caption / Subtitle Rule
- Always add auto-captions: **"Text"** → **"Auto Captions"** → tap **"Generate"**
- 85% of social media is watched without sound — captions are essential

### Export Settings
- **For Reels/TikTok:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30fps, MP4
- **For Facebook Post/Feed:** 1080×1080 (1:1), 30fps, MP4
- **For YouTube Shorts:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30fps, MP4
